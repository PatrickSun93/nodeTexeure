01

Executive summary

Noiraé has a genuinely elegant foundation. The homepage, shop and product pages already speak an Aesop-inspired luxury language — serif display type, generous whitespace, quiet monochrome buttons. The problem is not taste; it is consistency.

Underneath the storefront sit two separate design-system files that disagree with each other, and several pages ignore both and fall back to a generic template look. The journey changes personality screen to screen: a monochrome home, a teal shopping cart, an indigo registration form, and a gold accent that is defined in the tokens but never actually used. For a brand whose promise is refinement, that drift quietly erodes trust in exactly the places it matters most — the account and checkout flows.

02

What is working — keep it

The redesign should preserve and extend these strengths rather than start over. There is a real point of view here worth protecting.

Serif display voice

Cormorant-style headings give the marketing pages an editorial, gallery-like calm that suits silk and cashmere.

Restraint & whitespace

Home, shop and product pages breathe. Quiet monochrome buttons read as premium, not shouty.

A capable shop filter rail

Material, colour, price, size, brand and thread-count filters are a strong merchandising base — they just need balancing and polish.

Design tokens already exist

A full token file (colour, type, spacing, radius, shadow) is in place. The scaffolding for one system is there — it simply is not enforced.

04

Cross-cutting recommendations

Six moves that fix most pages at once, before any single screen is redrawn.

01

Collapse to one design system

Merge the two stylesheets into a single source of truth. One token set, imported everywhere; delete the duplicates so nothing can drift again.

02

Choose one accent — and one only

Pick a single accent (see the three directions in §06), retire teal and indigo entirely, and reserve any metallic strictly for a real VIP tier.

03

Fix authentication routing & copy

Merge /login and /signin, remove "Admin Login" from the customer path, and separate admin sign-in behind its own clearly labelled route.

04

Build one component kit

A single set of buttons, inputs, cards, badges and empty states — with defined hover, focus, disabled, loading, error and empty variants — used on every page.

05

Commit to real art direction

Replace every grey placeholder with consistent, warm-lit photography. A luxury textile brand lives or dies on the imagery.

06

Raise the accessibility floor

One visible focus style, AA-contrast text, 44px minimum hit targets, and honest loading/error states that never appear simultaneously.

05

Page-by-page audit

Each card lists what ships today, the specific issues found, and the recommended fix. Priority reflects revenue impact and how broken the current state is.

High priority Medium Low

Homepage  / 

High

Serif hero, two stacked collection tiles, featured products, brand story, three feature icons, newsletter.

Issues
  • Text-only hero on white — low impact for a visual brand; "Scroll to discover" floats awkwardly.
  • "Our Collections" title and its subtitle collide with no spacing.
  • "Featured Products," ships with a stray trailing comma.
  • Giant grey placeholders dominate; collection tiles stack full-width and tall with weak hierarchy.
Recommendations
  • Full-bleed photographic hero with restrained overlay text and a single primary CTA.
  • One reusable section-header pattern (eyebrow, title, subtitle) with fixed spacing.
  • Editorial 2-up collections; 3–4-up featured grid with hover quick-add.
  • Fix copy and replace placeholders with real photography.

Shop — listing & filters

High

Left filter rail (material, colour, price, size, brand, thread count), sort dropdown, three-column product grid.

Issues
  • Content capped at ~1000px, leaving a large empty right margin on desktop; layout reads left-heavy.
  • Price is a single-thumb slider — no minimum; colour swatches have no labels or selected state.
  • No result count or active-filter chips visible; placeholder imagery.
Recommendations
  • Widen the container and rebalance the grid; keep the rail sticky.
  • Dual-handle price range; named colour swatches with clear selected state.
  • Result count, active-filter chips with clear-all, and a mobile filter drawer.

Product detail

High

Single image, title, price, option chips, add-to-cart, trust badges, reviews block.

Issues
  • Reviews show "Network error. Please try again." and "No reviews yet" at the same time — two conflicting states shipped together.
  • Only one image, no gallery or zoom; an empty grey band sits above "Select options".
  • Thin for luxury bedding — no materials, care, dimensions or thread-count detail.
Recommendations
  • Image gallery with thumbnails and zoom; sticky buy box.
  • One review state at a time; graceful empty and error handling.
  • Details accordion (materials, care, dimensions, thread count) plus related products.

Cart

Medium

Empty state exists in two conflicting variants; filled cart with line items and summary.

Issues
  • Two empty states: one teal "Continue shopping" button jammed top-left, one centred black "Browse products".
  • Copy and vertical alignment differ between the two.
Recommendations
  • One canonical, centred empty state; serif heading, single primary CTA.
  • Filled cart with product thumbnails, qty steppers, sticky summary and a free-shipping progress bar.

Checkout

High

Multi-step flow (shipping, billing, payment, review) with Stripe; empty-cart guard.

Issues
  • No persistent order summary or clear step indicator to reassure the buyer.
  • Long single view; trust cues and inline validation are minimal.
Recommendations
  • Sticky order-summary rail and a clear progress stepper.
  • Guest checkout, express-pay options, inline validation and visible security cues.

Login & Sign-in two routes

High

Elegant serif card that matches the marketing pages — undermined by its copy and routing.

Issues
  • The customer login is titled "Admin Login" / "Sign in to access the admin dashboard".
  • Two overlapping routes (/login, /signin); register sends success to one and its "Sign in" link to the other.
  • No "create account" link or remember-me on the card.
Recommendations
  • One customer login ("Welcome back") that role-routes silently; admin sign-in on its own route.
  • Consolidate the routes; add register link, remember-me and clear error text.

Register worst offender

High

A generic Tailwind template form that shares none of the brand's language.

Issues
  • Indigo bg-indigo-600 button, bold sans heading, rounded-xl shadow-lg card — contradicts the whole brand.
  • Cramped field spacing; visually unrelated to the login page it sits beside.
Recommendations
  • Rebuild on the brand system — serif heading, hairline inputs, primary button matching login.
  • Add password-strength feedback, inline validation and a clear terms row.

Profile & account

High

Profile header, personal-information form, saved-addresses list.

Issues
  • The avatar overlaps the name and email — a positioning bug in the header.
  • A stray "0" renders beside the billing address; sections lack padding and hierarchy.
  • Inputs carry heavy shadows unlike the shop's hairline fields; no account navigation.
Recommendations
  • Account layout with side nav (Profile, Orders, Addresses, Wishlist, Security).
  • Fix the header, card-based address book, consistent hairline inputs, accent "Default" badge.
