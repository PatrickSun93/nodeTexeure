Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about ordering, care and returns.

Orders & shipping

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping arrives in 3–5 business days; express in 1–2. Orders over $150 ship complimentary, carbon-neutral, and dispatch within 24 hours.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout so there are no surprises on delivery.

Can I track my order?

Absolutely. You'll receive a tracking link by email, or track anytime from the Track order page.

Products & care

How do I wash silk bedding?

Machine wash cold on a delicate cycle with a silk-safe detergent, or dry clean. Lay flat or line dry away from direct sun; never bleach or tumble dry.

What thread count are your sheets?

Our silk is measured in momme (25mm), not thread count. Our cotton and linen range from 400 to 600 thread count, chosen for softness and longevity over marketing numbers.

Returns & guarantee

What is your 30-night trial?

Sleep on your bedding for 30 nights. If it isn't the best sleep of your life, return it for a full refund — no questions asked.

How do I start a return?

Start from My orders and select the item to return. We'll email a prepaid label and refund once it's on its way back.
