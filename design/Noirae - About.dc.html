Our Story

Crafted for the deepest rest

Noiraé began with a simple conviction: that the hours we spend asleep deserve the same care as the ones we spend awake. We set out to make bedding from the world's finest natural fibres — and nothing else.

Since 2019

From a single silk sheet

What started as one restless search for a better night's sleep became a small atelier devoted to natural-fibre bedding. We work directly with mills in Italy and Mongolia, choosing longer fibres, denser weaves and gentler finishes — the details you feel but rarely see.

Today Noiraé is still small by design. We'd rather make fewer things beautifully than many things quickly.

Natural fibres only

Mulberry silk, grade-A cashmere and Belgian linen — never synthetics.

Made responsibly

OEKO-TEX certified and traceable to mills that treat people well.

Kept simple

A tight, considered collection — no seasonal churn, no waste.
